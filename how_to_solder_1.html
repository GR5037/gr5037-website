Introduction

Soldering is a useful skill used to connect wires together by melting solder to create a strong electrical connection. This guide will introduce you to the basic tools, materials, and techniques needed to solder safely.

Safety First

Important Safety Notes

Soldering irons reach high temperatures and can cause burns or injusries if handled improperly. Always work in a well ventilated area to avoid breathing in the fumes. Safety glasses are requires when to protect your eyes from splashes of hot solder.
